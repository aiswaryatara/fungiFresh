Easy Recipe

Oyster Mushroom

A delicious oyster mushroom recipe that's quick and easy to prepare.

×

Oyster Mushroom

Ingredients

  • Oyster Mushrooms – 250g
  • Onion – 1 medium, finely chopped
  • Garlic – 3 cloves, minced
  • Green Chilli – 1, chopped
  • Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Turmeric Powder – ½ tsp
  • Red Chilli Powder – 1 tsp
  • Black Pepper – ½ tsp
  • Garam Masala – ½ tsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh Coriander – for garnish
×

King Oyster Mushroom

Recipe

Clean the king oyster mushrooms and cut them into thick slices. Heat olive oil and butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook until they become golden brown and tender. Season with salt, black pepper, Italian herbs, and chilli flakes. Toss everything well and cook for another 2–3 minutes. Finally, garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot as a delicious side dish or with your favorite meal.

Healthy Recipe

Shiitake Mushroom

A healthy and flavorful shiitake mushroom fry perfect for a quick meal.

×

Shiitake Mushroom

Ingredients

  • Shiitake Mushrooms – 250g
  • Olive Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Garlic – 3 cloves, finely chopped
  • Onion – 1 medium, sliced
  • Green Chilli – 1, chopped
  • Turmeric Powder – ½ tsp
  • Red Chilli Powder – ½ tsp
  • Black Pepper – ½ tsp
  • Soy Sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh Coriander – for garnish
×

Shiitake Mushroom

Recipe

Clean the shiitake mushrooms and remove the tough stems. Slice the mushrooms into thin pieces. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat and add the chopped garlic, onion, and green chilli. Sauté until the onion becomes soft and lightly golden. Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder, black pepper, and a little salt. Stir well and add the sliced shiitake mushrooms. Cook for 5–7 minutes until the mushrooms become tender and slightly crispy. Add soy sauce and mix everything together. Cook for another 1–2 minutes, then garnish with fresh coriander. Serve hot with rice, roti, bread, or as a healthy side dish.

Italian Recipe

King Oyster Mushroom

A rich and tasty king oyster mushroom fry that's perfect for lunch or dinner.

×

King Oyster Mushroom

Ingredients

  • King Oyster Mushrooms – 250g
  • Olive Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Garlic – 3 cloves, finely chopped
  • Onion – 1 medium, finely sliced
  • Bell Pepper – ½ cup, sliced
  • Italian Herbs – 1 tsp
  • Black Pepper – ½ tsp
  • Red Chilli Flakes – ½ tsp
  • Soy Sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Lemon Juice – 1 tsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh Parsley – for garnish
×

King Oyster Mushroom

Recipe

Clean the king oyster mushrooms thoroughly and remove the ends. Slice the mushrooms into thick, even pieces. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat and add the chopped garlic and sliced onion. Sauté until the onion becomes soft and lightly golden. Add the sliced bell pepper and cook for another 2–3 minutes. Add the Italian herbs, black pepper, red chilli flakes, and a little salt. Stir well and add the sliced king oyster mushrooms. Cook for 6–8 minutes until the mushrooms become tender and golden brown. Add soy sauce and lemon juice and mix everything together. Cook for another 1–2 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot with pasta, rice, bread, or as a delicious side dish.
